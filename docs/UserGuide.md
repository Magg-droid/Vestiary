# Wardrobe — User Guide

*A visual companion for Glamourer. Browse your designs as a gallery, organize them into collections, and let your outfits remember their Penumbra mods.*

---

## Getting Started

Open Wardrobe with `/wardrobe` or `/wr`.

Your Glamourer designs appear as visual cards. Double-click a card (or click **Apply**) to equip that outfit.

**Equipment-only mode:** Hold **Ctrl** while applying, or toggle it permanently in Settings.

---

## 🖼️ Thumbnails

Each design card shows a thumbnail. You can add custom thumbnails three ways:

| Icon | Action |
|---|---|
| 📷 | **Camera** — opens a 4:5 overlay. Move/resize the viewfinder, then capture |
| 📁 | **Upload** — pick an image file from your disk |
| 📋 | **Clipboard** — paste from clipboard (Win+Shift+S works great) |

Thumbnails are saved locally and persist across updates.

---

## 📁 Collections

Collections let you group designs into tabs based on Glamourer folders.

- **Create:** Click the **+** tab → name it → add folder paths (one per line)
- **Edit:** Right-click a tab → Edit
- **Reorder:** Drag and drop tabs
- **Delete:** Right-click → Delete

A collection without folders shows uncategorized designs (no Glamourer folder).

---

## ⭐ Favorites

Click the **star** icon on any design card to favorite it. A "Favorites" tab appears automatically. Un-favorite to remove it. Empty favorites tab disappears on its own.

---

## 🙈 Hide & Delete

| Icon | Action |
|---|---|
| 👁️ | **Eye** (header) — toggle to show/hide hidden designs |
| **Hide** | Remove from gallery without deleting from Glamourer |
| **Unhide** | Restore hidden design to gallery |
| **Delete** | Ctrl+Click → confirm → deletes from Glamourer permanently |

---

## 💾 Save Mods

Your outfits can remember their Penumbra mod setup. When you apply the outfit later, mods restore automatically.

### Setup
**Settings → Enable Save Mods** (one-time toggle)

### How to use
1. Open Penumbra, set up mods for the outfit
2. Click the **💾 icon** on the outfit card
3. Icon turns gold — mods saved
4. Apply the outfit anytime — mods restore automatically

### Controls
| Action | What |
|---|---|
| **Left-click 💾** | Save mods for this outfit |
| **Right-click 💾** | Clear saved mods |
| **Gold icon** | Mods saved |
| **Dim icon** | Not saved yet |

### Smart behavior
- **New mods** get turned OFF automatically — they won't mess up existing outfits
- **Deleted mods** auto-clean from saved state
- **Only equipment mods** are captured — hair/body/skin mods are left alone

---

## ⚙️ Settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Apply Equipment Only** | Skip customization when applying outfits |
| **Show Hidden** | Reveal hidden designs tab |
| **Enable Save Mods** | Turn on Penumbra mod saving (default: off) |
| **Theme** | Pick from Classic, Ocean, Midnight Purple, or Forest |

---

## 🎨 Themes

Four color themes available. Switch live in Settings — no reload needed.

| Theme | Vibe |
|---|---|
| **Classic** | Warm grey |
| **Ocean** | Cool blue-grey |
| **Midnight Purple** | Grey-lavender |
| **Forest** | Charcoal sage |

---

## 🔍 Search

The search bar in the header filters designs in the current tab by nickname or Glamourer name. Live filtering — just start typing.

---

## ✏️ Rename Designs

Double-click the design name below the thumbnail to set a nickname. Clear the nickname to revert to the Glamourer name.
